**What the Life of Jesus Shows Us About God**
================================================================================

Introduction  
We often wonder what God is really like—especially when life feels confusing or painful. Looking at Jesus’ life gives us a clear, loving picture of the Father’s character. This article walks through what Jesus reveals about God and how we can respond in everyday faith.

## Scripture Foundation  

Jesus himself said that seeing him is seeing the Father (John 14:9). In that verse he tells Philip, “Anyone who has seen me has seen the Father.” Scripture quotations are from the New International Version (NIV).  

He also described his unique relationship with the Father: “All things have been committed to me by my Father. No one knows the Son except the Father, and no one knows the Father except the Son and those to whom the Son chooses to reveal him” (Matthew 11:27). Scripture quotations are from the New International Version (NIV).  

These passages show that Jesus’ words and actions are not just teachings—they are the very expression of God’s heart. When we watch Jesus heal, forgive, and welcome the outcast, we see God’s compassion, justice, and mercy in action.

## What This Looks Like in Daily Life  

- **Seeing God’s love in kindness** – When we extend a hand to a neighbor in need, we mirror the way Jesus fed the hungry and healed the sick (Matthew 25:35‑40).  
- **Hearing God’s voice in humility** – Jesus washed his disciples’ feet, showing that greatness in God’s kingdom looks like service (John 13:14‑15).  
- **Trusting God’s faithfulness in hardship** – Even in Gethsemane, Jesus submitted to the Father’s will, teaching us to bring our anxieties to God in prayer (Luke 22:42).  
- **Extending God’s forgiveness** – Jesus forgave those who crucified him, inviting us to release bitterness and seek reconciliation (Luke 23:34).  

These patterns help us recognize God’s presence not only in spectacular moments but in the ordinary choices we make each day.

## Questions for Reflection  

1. When you think of God’s character, which attribute (love, justice, mercy, holiness) stands out most to you, and why?  
2. Recall a time you saw Jesus’ compassion in someone’s actions. How did that affect your view of God?  
3. In what ways might you be called to “wash feet” in your community this week?  
4. How does knowing Jesus reveals the Father change the way you approach prayer?  
5. What is one area where you find it hard to forgive, and how might Jesus’ example guide you?  
6. How can you practice seeing God’s presence in the ordinary tasks of your day?

## A Simple Practice This Week  

> **Practice:** Each morning, read one of Jesus’ actions from the Gospels (e.g., healing the blind man in John 9). Pause and ask, “What does this show me about God’s heart?” Write a brief note in a journal or on your phone about what you notice.  

A gentle next step: Share one insight from your reflection with a friend, small group, or in a prayer conversation, inviting them to see God through Jesus’ eyes together.

## Prayer  

Father, thank you for revealing yourself in Jesus. Help us to see your love in his compassion, your truth in his words, and your grace in his sacrifice. Teach us to follow his example, loving you and our neighbors with open hearts. In Jesus’ name we pray, Amen.

## Key Takeaways  

- Jesus’ life is the clearest picture of who God is.  
- Observing Jesus’ compassion, humility, obedience, and forgiveness shows us God’s character.  
- We can reflect God’s nature by loving others, serving humbly, trusting in hardship, and forgiving freely.  
- Small, regular practices—like reflecting on a Gospel story—help us grow in seeing God more clearly.
